Qone: Calculate the Volume of Earth
Write a program to compute the volume of Earth in km³ and miles³.
Hint: Volume of a sphere = (4/3) × π × r³; Earth's radius = 6378 km.
Output: The volume of earth in cubic kilometers is ____ and cubic miles is ____.

Qtwo: Find Harry's Age
Write a program to find the age of Harry if the birth year is 2000. Assume the current year is 2024.
Output: Harry's age in 2024 is ___.

Qthree: Calculate Sam’s Average Marks
Sam scored 94 in Maths, 95 in Physics, and 96 in Chemistry (out of 100). Find the average percent mark in PCM.
Output: Sam’s average mark in PCM is ___.

Qfour: Height Conversion
Write a program that takes your height in centimeters and converts it into feet and inches.
Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches, 1 inch = 2.54 cm.
Input: height
Output: Your Height in cm is ___ while in feet is ___ and inches is ___.

Qfive: Kilometers to Miles Conversion (Fixed Value)
Create a program to convert the distance of 10.8 kilometers to miles.
Hint: 1 km = 1.6 miles.
Output: The distance ___ km in miles is ___.

Qsix: Kilometers to Miles Conversion (User Input)

Input: km
Output: The total miles is ___ mile for the given ___ km.

Qseven: Distribute Pens Equally
Divide 14 pens among 3 students equally. Find how many pens each student will get and the remaining non-distributed pens.
Hint: Use modulus (%) for remainder.
Output: The Pen Per Student is ___ and the remaining pen not distributed is ___.

Qeight: Profit and Profit Percentage
Calculate the profit and profit percentage based on a cost price of INR 129 and selling price of INR 191.
Output:
The Cost Price is INR ___ and the Selling Price is INR ___
The Profit is INR ___ and the Profit Percentage is ___.
